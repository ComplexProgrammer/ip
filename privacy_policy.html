English

  • No User Account or Personal Data Collection: The application does not require registration and does not collect your name, phone number, contacts, files, or precise physical GPS location.
  • IP & Geolocation Lookups: When you look up an IP address or domain name, the query is processed via third-party geolocation APIs (ipapi.co or fallback ipwho.is) strictly to fetch geographical information (city, country, ISP, coordinates). When using the "My IP" feature, your device's public IP address is naturally visible to these lookup servers to return your IP info.
  • Local Storage: Your lookup history, pinned addresses, and app preferences are stored exclusively on your local device. You can clear your history at any time from the History screen. Uninstalling the app permanently erases all local data.
  • Third-Party Services & Advertising:
    • Google AdMob: Advertisements may be served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's Advertising ID to deliver relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
    • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: Anonymous crash reports and performance telemetry are collected via Google Firebase to detect stability issues and optimize application performance.
  • Network Diagnostics Tools: Diagnostic utilities (Ping, DNS lookup, WHOIS, Port Scanner) initiate standard network connections directly from your device to the target host specified by you.
